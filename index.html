About

I'm Alberto Olmo, a Computer Science Ph.D. who loves turning machine learning and deep learning into working solutions for research and enterprise projects. I did my Ph.D. at Arizona State University under Prof. Subbarao Kambhampati, studying where and why large deep learning models fail, from large language models and computer vision systems to automated planning.
