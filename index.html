About

My name is Alberto Olmo, I am a fourth year Computer Science Ph.D. candidate at Arizona State University under the supervision of prof. Subbarao Kambhampati at the Yochan lab. Previously, I obtained a Master of Science in Computer Science at the University of Colorado at Colorado Springs and a Bachelor's in Computer Science at Universitat Autònoma de Barcelona.

My broad research interests lie in explainability of machine learning models and data biases.
